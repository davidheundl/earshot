# The Threat Class is per Scenario; poachers are vehicles off the tracks

Earshot hunted drones only: the Belief confirmed, followed and merged drone-class Tracks, the Policy verified only drone Suspicions, and RF rings always raised the drone class. To guard a game reserve against poachers with the same swarm (David, 2026-10-20), a Scenario now names its Threat Class (`threat:`, default drone), every drone knows it before take-off (`SwarmConfig.threat`), and everything that meant "the intruder" reads it instead of `TargetClass.DRONE`. Off the places the City explains it, the Threat Class gets the drone's prior (-5) rather than its own lower one (a vehicle off a road: -8), so an engine in the bush is as suspicious as a drone over the apron; on a track it stays Explained, as trucks on a road always were.

Rejected: a fifth Target Class (Poacher). Class probabilities are packed as 4 bytes in every Observation, and CONFUSION, ACOUSTICS, the waveform class folders, the sound classifier's fold and the posterior are sized by the four classes; a poacher's pickup also sounds and propagates like any vehicle, so the class would have to copy Vehicle everywhere. Rejected too: relabelling a slot per world (Drone meaning "poacher" in the reserve), which would give a pickup a drone's acoustics.

What separates a poacher from a ranger in the map is only where it drives. A poacher that keeps to the tracks is Explained like a game drive and never verified; a ranger off the tracks is verified, and then only the camera tells them apart: the stand-in knows the reserve's own vehicles (their livery), the COCO detector in Webots does not. Explaining the rangers wherever they drive would need their radios' GPS positions as City knowledge; not built.

RF bands name their emitter class (`Band.emitter`): the drone links raise the drone ring, the new UHF band (handhelds, push-to-talk) the vehicle ring. Ground truth marks intruders (`intruder:`), so metrics count poachers and not rangers.

Followed by changes any world sees (judged with `earshot eval`, see [savanna.md](../savanna.md)): Explained Tracks are sent one hop, at most one per 300 m per 30 s, and take their own six places in a map's Track list instead of crowding out the Suspicions; a vehicle is Explained by a road through its uncertainty ellipse, not only within 60 m; and a Digest names only the frames worth healing (Claims, Tracks not Explained), because a drone 10 km away never hears another's one-hop Observations and kept asking for them. With twenty drones a ranger's radio made a trail of Explained peaks that filled every drone's list, and healing requests filled the rest of the channel.
