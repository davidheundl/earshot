# Each sensor's evidence counts as at most 1.5 independent readings

A static microphone sends a bearing every second, but its errors are correlated (same position, same reflections, same noise), so summing its log-likelihoods makes one sensor arbitrarily confident and a single bearing line turns into a Suspicion. We first capped the summed log-odds per drone and sensor with tanh; that flattened the wedge so cells two error-widths off a bearing scored like cells on it, which made every blob ~150 m wide and produced Ghosts everywhere. Instead each (drone, sensor family) contributes its summed log-likelihood G scaled by min(1, N_EFF / W), where W is how many readings covered the cell: the sum while there are few readings, their average times N_EFF after. G and W are plain sums, so the map stays independent of arrival order (concept 8.4), and the wedge keeps its shape. With N_EFF = 1.5 and a drone prior of -5 log-odds, one bearing line alone cannot make a Suspicion, two crossing lines make a sharp one, and two RF rings make a coarse one.

Amended 2026-10-11: G and W decay by the same factor, so G · N_EFF / W never faded once a sensor fell silent; Suspicions outlived their Source by ~90 s. The cap is now N_EFF · exp(-(t − newest reading of that drone and family) / TAU), still order-independent because the newest time is a max.
