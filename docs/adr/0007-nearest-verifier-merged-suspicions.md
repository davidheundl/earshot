# One Verifier per danger, the nearest; overlapping Suspicions are one

Concept section 7 sent up to two more drones per Suspicion to roofs whose bearings would cross the existing ones, then verified once the fix was tight, with bids that weighed confidence against the Gap a listener leaves. At peaks every drone was away from its post (6 of 6 in the demo, 10 of 10 in the valley), and each drone's map kept several Ghosts beside the true crossing. We decided instead (David, 2026-10-11): the nearest drone verifies and everyone else holds their post to catch further dangers; overlapping drone Suspicions, in a drone's own map and in received Tracks, merge into one at their likelihood-weighted mean (weight exp(log-odds), so equally strong zones average evenly and a much weaker Ghost barely pulls). Bids are distance only and the auction is by place (Claims within 300 m are one danger), because each drone's averaged Suspicion can fall in a different 100 m cell; Tracks carry their log-odds so a drone that knows a Suspicion only from a neighbour can still win as the nearest. The cost: nothing sharpens a poor fix any more, so a Verifier sometimes arrives more than the camera's 100 m from the drone and is dismissed. On 2026-10-11, demo 11/12 confirmed (median 46 s), random 9/12 (43 s), valley 4/4 (262 s), with at most 2 drones away at once; before: 12/12 (87 s), 11/12 (82 s), 4/4 (178 s) with 6 and 10 away.
