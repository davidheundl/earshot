# Policy tuning, first pass (2026-10-18)

`earshot tune`, 30 generations × 12 candidates × 10 training seeds (random worlds 0-9), CMA-ES
over the eight `PolicyParams` genes. 2 h 10 min on 7 workers (about 14 s per run in parallel).
Result: [scenarios/params/tuned-2026-10-18.json](../scenarios/params/tuned-2026-10-18.json),
use with `--params`.

## What changed

| Param | Default | Tuned | |
|---|---|---|---|
| verify_min_strength | 4.0 | 4.93 | barely moved |
| verify_min_confidence | 0.5 | 0.21 | at the lower bound |
| top_suspicions | 2 | 3 | |
| confirmed_shadow | 200 m | 359 m | ghosts near a confirmed drone ignored |
| settle | 3.0 s | 5.1 s | |
| same_task_radius | 200 m | 287 m | more Suspicions count as one danger |
| arrive | 60 m | 45 m | at the lower (protocol) bound |
| dismiss_after | 8 s | 12.3 s | |

The optimiser did not raise the strength gate. It halved wasted flights by merging Suspicions
into fewer dangers and dwelling longer before letting go: the ghost crossings were being
re-verified.

## Caveats

- **One intruder per world.** `scenario_gen` makes exactly one drone, so wider merge radii
  were free in training. `two_drones.yaml` (second drone ~340 m from the first, arriving after
  it is confirmed) shows the second drone is missed by *both* sets (9/10 and 10/10): a blind
  spot of the policy, not of the tuning. A generator with several intruders is the next step
  before trusting larger radii.
- **Not converged.** The best set came from generation 14 and the generation mean never fell
  much: sigma 0.25 with 12 candidates explores a noisy landscape. Two genes sit at their bounds.
- **Perception ceiling.** About 15 % of random worlds (3 of 20 held-out) are never detected by
  either set: no Suspicion, no flight. That is the sensor model, not the decisions.
- **Seed 1002**, the one new "miss": the tuned set confirms after 22 s (default 68 s) but 108 m
  from the drone, just outside the 100 m match radius; the 359 m shadow then stops any
  correction.

## Should 18b become the default? (2026-10-19: no)

`tuned-2026-10-18b.json` (re-tuned after 49f0295 on worlds with a second intruder in 30 %)
against the code defaults, paired, after the belief fix:

| Check | Better / worse | False verifies | Verify flights | Missed | t_confirm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| random, 40 new seeds (2000-2039) | 25 / 5 | 18.5 → 5.1 | 23.7 → 8.7 | 22 → 25 | 158 → 171 s |
| demo.yaml, 10 seeds | 10 / 0 | 24.7 → 7.7 | 30.3 → 13.2 | 0 → 0 | 43 → 41 s |
| two_drones.yaml, 20 seeds | 13 / 7 | 21.7 → 6.1 | 34.0 → 14.3 | 2 → 8 | 32 → 37 s |
| muc.yaml, 5 seeds | 3 / 2 | 28.4 → 0.6 | 31.2 → 1.4 | 1 → 2 | 221 → 284 s |

It cuts false verifies to a quarter everywhere but misses more drones in three of four checks,
which fails the rule that a tuned set must miss no more than the default. The code defaults
stay; use 18b only with `--params`, and only where it misses nothing (demo.yaml).
