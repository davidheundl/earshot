## The one decision to make first

**The demo script no longer matches the policy.** Concept 16 at 1:20 says "a third drone perches at a right angle, the blob shrinks to ~100 m", and key message 1 is "the drone decides how well it wants to hear." Since commit "Remove listening tasks and roof choice" (Oct 11/12) the policy is nearest-Verifier-only: perched drones hold their home roof, nobody picks a Perch Site by crossing angle. DECLARATION.md still lists "perch-site selection" under *Built on the day*.

Recommendation: make **information-driven perching** the headline on-the-day component (idea A1). It is the most creative part of the concept, it is the thing no competitor in concept 2.3 does, Thrun will recognise it as active sensing, and it can close the time-to-confirm gap below. If it is not built, rewrite beat 1:20 and key message 1 before the rehearsal.

## Weak spots the judges will find

- **Earshot confirms slower than the naive baseline.** `runs/eval-random.json` (30 runs): `earshot` 107 s median to confirm vs. `nearest` 44 s; `earshot` wins on unnecessary flights (4.5 vs. 31) and localisation error (62 vs. 88 m). Either close the gap (A1, A3) or present it as a trade with the numbers on the slide: "one flight instead of 30, ~1 minute later."
- **The baselines look like straw men.** `no_perch` confirms 5/30. A judge may ask why not "nearest drone flies, but with our map" (explaining away on, perching on, no strength gate / no auction). Adding that baseline (D2) makes the win credible and isolates what the decision layer contributes.
- **Battery is a quiet win; use it.** Battery used per drone on random scenarios: `earshot` 17 %, `nearest` 21 %, `no_perch` 24 %. That backs "perching saves energy" with a number; convert it to minutes of endurance for the slide.
