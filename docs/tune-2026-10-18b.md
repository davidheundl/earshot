# Policy tuning, second pass: worlds with two intruders (2026-10-18)

`earshot tune --train 20 --gens 20 --holdout 50`, CMA-ES over the eight `PolicyParams` genes, same
cost and bounds as the [first pass](tune-2026-10-18.md). What changed is the training set:
`scenario_gen` now puts a second intruder 250-500 m from the first in 30 % of random worlds, and
belief.py no longer hides a second drone behind a confirmed one (commit 49f0295). Seeds 0-9 hold
only 2 such worlds, seeds 0-19 hold 5, so this pass trains on 20 seeds and runs 20 generations
instead of 30 (the first pass found its best set in generation 14). 4 h on 7 workers.
Result: [scenarios/params/tuned-2026-10-18b.json](../scenarios/params/tuned-2026-10-18b.json),
use with `--params`.

## What changed

| Param | Default | 2026-10-18 | 2026-10-18b | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| verify_min_strength | 4.0 | 4.93 | 6.33 | a stricter strength gate |
| verify_min_confidence | 0.5 | 0.21 | 0.75 | |
| top_suspicions | 2 | 3 | 1 | one Suspicion at a time |
| confirmed_shadow | 200 m | 359 m | 272 m | narrower than the 340 m gap in two_drones |
| settle | 3.0 s | 5.1 s | 4.4 s | |
| same_task_radius | 200 m | 287 m | 275 m | |
| arrive | 60 m | 45 m | 112 m | |
| dismiss_after | 8 s | 12.3 s | 8.3 s | |

The first set cut false verifies by verifying many weak Suspicions and ignoring everything near a
confirmed drone. The new set does it the other way round: it verifies few, strong, confident
Suspicions and keeps the shadow narrower, so a second drone 250-500 m away stays visible.

## Reading it

- On the generator's worlds the second drone is priced in: with two intruders the new set misses
  9 of 20 drones, against 13 (default) and 12 (2026-10-18), with fewer false verifies than either.
  Single-intruder worlds do not pay for it (10 → 11 → 9).
- On two_drones.yaml seeds 10-19 it misses 5 second drones where the default misses none. That
  scenario's second drone arrives after the first is confirmed, about 340 m away, heard by RF
  only (every listener is ~600 m away). None of the 5 training worlds with two intruders is like
  that, and no single gene accounts for the misses (seeds 10-19, new set with one gene changed):

  | change | missed | false verifies |
  |---|---|---|
  | none | 5 | 6.4 |
  | top_suspicions 2 | 3 | 5.9 |
  | verify_min_strength 4.0 | 3 | 14.0 |
  | confirmed_shadow 200 m | 2 | 11.0 |
  | verify_min_confidence 0.5 | 5 | 6.4 |
  | arrive 60 m | 4 | 6.6 |

  A 272 m shadow still costs drones 340 m apart, probably because the confirmed point sits
  65-115 m off the drone (loc_err in these runs), so the gap the shadow sees can be under 272 m.
- Both tuned sets are opt-in (`--params`); the default is unchanged. For mixed worlds the new
  set is the better choice. The late RF-only second drone of two_drones.yaml is not something
  more tuning on this generator fixes: it needs a way to tell two co-band emitters apart (a
  listener in hearing range, or an RF model with channel/ID), or a generator case that matches it.

## Caveats

- **Not converged.** The best set came from generation 20, the last one, and the generation mean
  never fell below ~457. A longer run may still move.
- **Thin second-drone signal.** 5 two-intruder worlds in training, 10 in the held-out check.
- **muc.yaml regressed** (missed 1 → 2, t_confirm 221 → 284 s) on 5 seeds: weak evidence, but
  the stricter gates slow confirmation there.
- **Perception ceiling.** As before, some random worlds are never detected by any set.
