## Processes (live mode)

                     │ dashboard  FastAPI :8000, WebSocket /ws, read-only  │
                     └────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

With `--backend gazebo` the box at the top is Gazebo Sim instead: `gz_bridge.py` (Homebrew Python, `gazebo/.venv`) embeds the Gazebo server and answers the world's REQ on `:5554`, one round trip per tick; `gz sim -g` shows it (ADR 0008).

`earshot live` starts all of these and stops them together. `earshot fast` runs the same World, RadioMedium and Agents in one process in lockstep, with no sockets (ADR 0001).

The live world outlives its script: after the Scenario's `duration` the scripted Sources have ended, but the swarm, the radio and operator Events keep running until Ctrl-C, or for `--duration` sim seconds (`world_proc.py:167-171`). If Webots quits mid-run (window closed, crash), `WebotsBackend` says so once and keeps stepping its own 2D twin at wall-clock pace, without camera images (`webots.py:280-323`); the run, the dashboard and the HUD carry on.

### Perception: where the models run

Trained models run in the world process only. Agents get `SensorReading`s, never audio or images: `Sensed` has no image field; the World turns the Verifier's frame into CAMERA readings and keeps the frame itself (`World.camera`, for the detector and the preview) (ADR 0001).

- `sensors.build_perception(scenario, classifier=, sounds=, audio=, camera=, renders=)` (`sensors/__init__.py:376`) returns `(Audio | None, YoloDetector | None)` from the Scenario's `audio:` and `camera:` keys, overridden by the command line (`--classifier none` and `--camera truth` turn them off). A model that cannot load (no torch or ultralytics, missing weights) is reported and replaced by its stand-in; it never stops a run.
- Sound: `audio: {classifier: "mod:attr", sounds: dir}`. With a classifier, every sound window is rendered as audio and each detected Source's beam is classified (`earshot.audio.classifier:efficientat`); without one, the `CONFUSION` table stands in.
- Camera: `camera: {detector: yolo, verify_conf, confirm_frames, clear_radius, clear_windows, every, imgsz, device, weights, resolution, standoff}`. Only Webots renders images the detector reads (`renders=a.backend == "webots"`, `world_proc.py:124-127`); the 2D twin and Gazebo keep the stand-in. The World sets `backend.detector_frames = True` (`world.py:50-51`), so `WebotsBackend.camera_frame` returns `{"bgra", "w", "h", "fov" (rad), "yaw" (deg), "pitch" (rad), "height" (m above the ground), "objects"}` on every Verifier tick (10 Hz, `VERIFY_PERIOD_MS`); `every: 2` runs inference on every other frame. `objects` (Webots' recogniser) is still there; the YOLO path ignores it.
- `camera.resolution: [w, h]` sets the Verifier camera's size in the generated Webots world (`tools/build_webots_world.py:1838-1840`; default 400x240). `camera.standoff` sets how close the Verifier hovers to the point it looks at (`Kinematics.for_scenario`, `twin2d.py:40-51`; default 40 m), in all backends.

Live flags: `earshot live` takes `--audio`, `--sounds DIR`, `--classifier MOD:ATTR|none` and `--camera yolo|truth`, which go to the world process, and `--params FILE`, which goes to every agent process. The Scenario's `audio:` and `camera:` are the defaults; the flags override them (`cli.py:497-508`, `world_proc.py:104-110`).

`scenarios/muc_full.yaml` is the integrated Munich scenario: EfficientAT head for sound, YOLO on a 1280x768 Verifier view, 25 m standoff.

### Second world: the savanna Reserve

`scenarios/savanna.yaml` runs the same swarm against poachers in a 10 x 10 km game reserve ([savanna.md](savanna.md)). Nothing above the Backend seam knows which world runs; what differs is data:
- `threat: vehicle` (Scenario, then `SwarmConfig.threat`): the Threat Class the Belief confirms, follows and merges and the Policy verifies. Off the places the City explains it, it gets the threat prior (-5), so an engine off the tracks is a Suspicion and one on a track is Explained (ADR 0009). Default drone: Munich, the valley and the demo are unchanged.
- The UHF band (`sensors.rf.BANDS["uhf"]`, `Sensor.RF_UHF`): handhelds, push-to-talk `duty` 0.4, its ring on the Vehicle class (`Band.emitter`).
- Sources carry `intruder:` (metrics, Threat Log; default: the Threat Class) and `kind:` (the camera stand-in's label, the Webots model). `confusion:` overrides the sound stand-in's rows (game heard as an engine).
- Guide (demo mock, `guide: true`; `scenarios/muc_demo.yaml`): `World._guide` gives a Verifier in VERIFY the true position of the intruder within 600 m of its goal as `Sensed.guide`, the one exception to "truth never reaches an Agent" (ADR 0001). The Policy flies there at `follow_max_speed`, yields to a nearer guided Verifier, confirms on a camera sighting or 3 s after arriving, and its Quarry takes the guide as it is. Sound and RF still find the intruder and the auction still dispatches; never evaluate with it.
- `params.follow_for`: a Verifier stays with what it confirmed (Follow) and reports "following" once a second, with its Quarry's speed (`v`). Its Quarry (`agent/follow.py`, an alpha-beta filter) fuses its own camera hits, which `Agent.step` hands the Policy as `sightings`, with the Confirmed Track: a hit with `SensorReading.range` is a point fix, a bare bearing takes its range from the prediction. The range stays on the drone (never on the radio, never in the Belief): a geolocated vehicle (`camera.range`), or a drone's box size (`camera.box_range`, a 0.4 m quadcopter, in Webots; the stand-in: the true distance times a fixed 0.85-1.15 per Source, its unknown real size). Without it a Follower falls behind a drone that flies away from it: the camera cannot see that direction. The Follower faces the Quarry (`look_at`, so the Verifier camera stays on it) and asks for `Command.speed` = the Quarry's speed + `follow_gain` per metre beyond `follow_gap`, at most `follow_max_speed`; `Kinematics.max_speed` caps it in every backend. The camera's pitch stays fixed: it turns with the heading only.
- Shots (`{op: shot, source: poacher2}`, `Scenario.shots`): a rifle fired is an Event, not a Source. `SensorSuite._shots` gives each drone one `Sensor.SHOT` reading when the sound arrives (distance / 343 m/s), with `acoustics.SHOT` (heard for kilometres, bearing 2-6 deg) and its own random stream, so a run is unchanged until a Shot is heard; class probabilities on the Threat Class, as an RF band's on its emitter. The Belief folds it as a sound bearing in its own family `(origin, "shot")`, gated by `SHOT_HEARING_RANGE` and only positive: in the tower's `"sound"` family thirty engine and animal bearings would average it away. A Source may stop on its path (`[x, y, s]`, `Source.waits`): poacher2 stands at its kill.
- `threat_log.ThreatLog` in both runners: one entry per checked danger, `runs/*.threats.jsonl`, `state.threats` for the dashboard.
- The City (`tools/make_savanna.py`, `biome: savanna`, `road_surfaces`) and the Webots builder's bushveld look. `camera.classes` maps any YOLO model's labels to Target Classes; the reserve keeps the stand-in (`detector: truth`): COCO `yolo11n.pt` sees Webots' Range Rovers as cars only 50-160 m ahead and missed the moving poacher live (its primitive pickups read as airplane or chair: `data/cv/savanna-vehicles-20261021/REPORT.md`). With a detector reading the frames the Verifier camera's tilt aims at the hunted class's height at the standoff (`WebotsBackend._verify_pitch`, `SOURCE_Z`: a drone 10 m below, a vehicle on the ground; the stand-in keeps the drone's tilt and sees out to `camera.range`), and with `camera.range` a YOLO hit is geolocated from its box's bottom edge and the frame's `height` (`camera.ground_range`), as the stand-in's are.
- `camera.range` (300 m): the stand-in sees a vehicle as far as a thermal camera does and geolocates it (a hit's `level` is its range), so the confirmed point and a Follower sit on the vehicle, not on the Verifier.
- `params.max_verify_distance` (3000 m) and `radio.relay_radius_m` (4000 m), both off by default: no drone bids on a task further off, and Tracks and Claims are relayed only by drones near the place they are about. Without them, Claims lost on the busy channel sent drones 7 km across the reserve and every Track flooded all twenty.
- Twenty drones: `map_cell` 50 m, `radio.nominal_range_m` 3500. For any world: Explained Tracks go one hop, at most one per 300 m per 30 s, and no longer take the Suspicions' six places in a map's Track list; a Digest names only frames worth healing (Claims, Tracks not Explained), so drones stop asking for one-hop frames they can never get. A drone's readings of one sensor window share one Observation, up to two per 40 B frame (68 -> 63.5 % load in the reserve).

|---|---|---|

| `city` | `City.load(json)`, `obstacles_between(a, b, za, zb)`, `ground(x, y)`, `distance_to_rail/road`, `grid` | Static prior. Schema in the module docstring. |

Every Event (YAML, hotkey, replay) goes through `apply`; op list in the module docstring. Optional keys `params:` (PolicyParams), `audio:`, `camera:` (see Perception), `threat:` (the Threat Class), `confusion:`; per Source `intruder:`, `kind:`. |
| `backend` | `step(dt, commands) -> states`, `camera_frame(id)`, `close()` | Adapters, chosen by `--backend twin2d\|webots\|gazebo` (`world_proc.make_backend`): `twin2d.Twin2D`, `webots.WebotsBackend` (ADR 0002), `gazebo.GazeboBackend` (ADR 0008; talks to `gz_bridge.py`, which embeds Gazebo Sim in its own Python). All three fly the drones with `Twin2D(city, drones, Kinematics.for_scenario(scenario))`. `WebotsBackend.detector_frames` (set by the World) adds the image and camera geometry to each Verifier frame. |
| `sensors` | `SensorSuite(city, scenario, rng, audio, camera).sense(state, t, frame) -> [Detection]`, `build_perception(...)` | Physics in `acoustics.py` / `rf.py` (pure), calibrated to concept 5.1/5.2 by `tests/test_sensors.py`. A Detection = reading + truth; truth never reaches Agents. Camera: with a `YoloDetector` and a rendered frame, its multi-frame verdict decides; else Webots' recogniser (`frame["objects"]`), else ground truth with a 90 % detection rate within 100 m. |
| `sensors.camera` | `YoloDetector.observe(frame)` every rendered tick, `.verdict() -> CameraVerdict(hits, clear_radius)` once per 1 s window, `.reset()`, `load_detector(spec)`, `box_bearing` | TomSmail/drone-yolo-v1 (`data/cv/verifier-demo-20261015/evaluation/drone-best.pt`) with ByteTrack. A hit needs the same track at `verify_conf` for `confirm_frames` processed frames in a row; "nothing here" only after `clear_windows` windows without any quadcopter box, and only out to `clear_radius`; anything else is no reading. Needs `uv sync --group cv`; `EARSHOT_CV_DEVICE` picks the device (default mps if available). |
| `sensors.waveform` | `ClipBank(dir)`, `Mixer.window(...) -> AudioWindow`, `Mixer.beam(win, i)`, `load_classifier("mod:attr")` | Off unless asked (`--audio`, `--classifier`, or a Scenario's `audio.classifier`). Each 1 s sound window rendered as 16 kHz audio: recordings from `data/sounds/<drone\|vehicle\|train\|nothing\|ego\|background>/*.wav`, synthetic loops for classes without any; every part scaled to the level `acoustics` computes, so waveform SNR = logged SNR. A classifier (`classify(audio, sr) -> 4 probs` by TargetClass) gets one *beam* per detected Source (it + the others 6 dB down + noise), not the raw mix, and replaces the `CONFUSION` stand-in. Draws no random numbers: with audio on, everything else in the run stays the same (a classifier aside). `sensors.export.AudioExport` writes training data (`earshot fast --audio DIR [--audio-beams]`): `drone<k>.wav`, `labels.jsonl`, `beams/`, `meta.json`. |
| `world` | `World(scenario, city, backend, audio, camera)`, `World.step(commands) -> {id: Sensed}`, `detections`, `audio`, `snapshot()`, `camera_preview()` | Backend + truth + sensors. Runners own the clock. `detections` feeds logs and metrics. |
| `radio.medium` | `RadioMedium.step(t, positions, outgoing, jam) -> {rx: [(frame, margin_db)]}`, `stats()` | LoRa airtime, shadowing drawn once per (frame, receiver), half duplex, capture against the summed power of all overlapping frames, duty cycle, per-sender priority queue (Claim > Request > Track > Observation > Digest), start jitter drawn once per frame. |
| `agent` | `Agent.step(t, sensed, inbox) -> StepOut` | Pure. Composes `MessageStore`, `Belief`, `Policy`. |
| `agent.belief` | `Belief(city, cell, threat)`, `add_evidence`, `tracks(t)`, `check_faults(t)`, `posterior()` | Probability Map (threat: the Threat Class; what "drone" means below): bounded log-likelihood per (origin, sensor family), at most N_EFF readings' worth each (ADR 0006); rail/road priors explain trains and trucks; camera confirms or dismisses (a "nothing" reading clears the radius it carries); a confirmed point follows only its nearest drone peak within 150 m, at most 15 m/s, and other peaks that close are Explained as its side lobes (`_follow_confirmed`); overlapping drone Suspicions merge at their likelihood-weighted mean, Confirmed only with Confirmed (`merge_overlapping`, also applied to received Tracks); Faulty Sensor check. |

| `agent.store` | `receive(frame, margin, t)`, `due(t)`, `on_digest`, `on_request` | Duplicate filter; contention-based relaying (far receivers first, cancel on overhearing, skip when heard strongly); Digest/Request healing of Claims and Tracks that are not Explained (`healable`; own frames count in the Digest); schemes `ours` / `flood` / `unicast3`. |
| `eval` | `evaluate(scenario, runs, seed, policy, scheme, bw, params, audio, jobs, duration)`, `sound_audio(classifier)`, `summarize` | Section 17 metrics from decision events + ground truth; `scenario_gen` makes a random world per seed. Latencies are censored: a drone Source never confirmed (or dispatched to) counts as the scenario's `duration`, averaged over every drone Source in the run; `loc_err` covers every dispatch. `jobs` > 1 runs seeds in parallel with the same rows (not with a classifier). `earshot eval` builds sound only from `--classifier`, not from a Scenario's `audio:`. |
| `tune` | `tune(scenario, gens, pop, train, holdout, workers, sigma)`, `run_cost(m)` | `earshot tune`: CMA-ES over the `GENES` of `PolicyParams` (bounded so the protocol stays safe), cost = t_confirm (censored mean over drone Sources) + 600 per missed drone + 10 per false verify + 10 per drone away at once, on fixed training seeds (0..), then default vs best paired on held-out seeds (1000..). Writes `runs/tune-<time>/` (history.jsonl, best.json, holdout.json). `outbid_cooldown` is a PolicyParam but not a gene. |
| `audio` | `ArraySensor(device, rotation, classifier=None).readings()`, `doa.estimate_bearing` | Real microphone arrays as a perched drone's sound sensor (SRP-PHAT, 500-4000 Hz); class from a sound classifier if given, else the coarse spectral stand-in. |
| `audio.classifier` | `zeroshot`, `efficientat` (for `--classifier earshot.audio.classifier:<name>`), `FINE`, `fold` | Needs the `ml` group (torch) and `data/models/` (tools/fetch_efficientat.py for the backbone; the trained head `sound_head.pt` is committed). Inference only, no random numbers. `EARSHOT_SOUND_DEVICE=mps` for speed. |
| `threat_log` | `ThreatLog(threat)`, `add(event)`, `look(detections)`, `lines()`, `entries()` | Runner side, like `VerifyCounts`: one entry per danger a Verifier checked (confirmed with the camera's label and the followed path, or dismissed with what was seen instead). `lockstep.run` writes `<log>.threats.jsonl` and `RunResult.threats`; `world_proc` writes `runs/live-*.threats.jsonl` and `state.threats`. |

## Pre-built vs. on the day

Rule (concept section 14): infrastructure is built beforehand and declared ([DECLARATION.md](../DECLARATION.md)); the judged intelligence is built on Oct 24. Branch `main` holds the infrastructure and a working version of the judged logic, built before the event to rehearse and measure: using it on the day means declaring it (DECLARATION.md lists it), or rebuilding it on the day. Branch `integrate` is the merge of `prototype` (the swarm), `main` (Gazebo backend) and `feat/cv-applications` (offline CV), with the trained sound classifier and the YOLO Verifier detector wired in.

Open items worth doing on the day:
- Ghosts beside a confirmed drone: its side lobes within 150 m are Explained now (`_follow_confirmed`), but bearings that point at it still cross other bearings further out and make Ghosts.
- A quieter channel, so the swarm also runs on a 125 kHz LoRa channel (it needs 500 kHz now). A window's readings already share one Observation (2026-10-21), but that was a quarter of the frames: at 125 kHz the demo still hits the 10 % duty cycle (9/12 confirmed, no agreement after the jam). In the reserve the rest was healing (resends and Requests, 29 % of the frames), relays (22 %) and own Tracks (19 %) ([savanna.md](savanna.md#results-in-the-reserve)).
- Realism: airport background noise, honest RF, a camera stand-in with the detector's measured range (see [functionality.md](functionality.md#known-limitations-and-realism) and [review-2026-10-18.md](review-2026-10-18.md) section 3).

## Events and replay

Live Events are applied at the next world tick, tagged `live`, and recorded to `runs/live-<time>.events.jsonl`. `earshot fast --events <file>` replays a recorded session on top of its scenario, deterministically, so a demo rehearsal can become a regression run or an evaluation scenario.

## Gotchas

- A ZeroMQ SUB socket returns the oldest queued message. Drain with `NOBLOCK` to get the newest (see `agent_proc.py`).
- Every context sets `LINGER 0`, or processes hang on exit waiting for unsent messages.
- Webots needs `WEBOTS_HOME`, `PYTHONPATH` and `DYLD_LIBRARY_PATH` (`cli.webots_env()`); our instance runs on port 1240 so an already open Webots does not grab the controller. `earshot live` waits up to 60 s for the RIG's extern-controller socket before starting the world.
- Webots Preferences → OpenGL → Texture quality must be High on every Mac that shows the demo: below High, Webots halves every texture of 1024 px or more (the Munich photo turns to mush). Anti-aliasing on and ambient occlusion Medium also help; shadows stay off.
- Regenerate the Webots world after changing the City, the scenario's sources or its `camera.resolution`: `earshot live --backend webots` does this automatically (the Munich world takes a few minutes the first time).
- `WebotsBackend` sleeps each basic step to its wall-clock deadline. With `--no-webots-view`, `earshot live` starts Webots in fast mode, so our per-tick work fits into that slack (1.0x); with the 3D window it stays in realtime mode, where our work comes on top of every step. YOLO inference on the Verifier view also comes on top: keep `every: 2` or raise it if the run falls behind.
- Each Webots camera renders the whole scene. The view cameras run only while a game screen sends its `/view` keepalive (every 2 s, not from the evidence screen or a hidden tab); `--no-webots-view` skips Webots' own 3D window, the largest remaining cost.
- With `detector_frames`, the Verifier frame carries a full BGRA image every tick (1280x768x4 ≈ 3.9 MB). It stays in the world process; never put it on a socket.
- `earshot eval --jobs N` cannot share a loaded sound classifier: with `--classifier` it runs serially.
- Gazebo: every gz process needs `GZ_IP=127.0.0.1` and `GZ_PARTITION=earshot` (`cli.gazebo_env()`), or another Gazebo on the network joins our bus. To look at a running world from a shell: `GZ_IP=127.0.0.1 GZ_PARTITION=earshot gz topic -l`.
- Gazebo's label render (the Verifier's box camera) costs per visual in the scene: merge geometry into few meshes. An OBJ without normals renders white; the builder adds them.
- Two live runs on one Mac share the same ports: `earshot live` refuses to start while any of them listens. It cannot see a run that is still starting up, so one Mac (or one checkout's session) runs one live session at a time.
